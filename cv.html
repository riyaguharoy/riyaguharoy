Clinical-Profile-Conditioned Synthetic Cardiac MRI

Master's thesis at USN with Simula Research Laboratory, part of the EU-funded SEARCH project on trustworthy synthetic healthcare data.

  • Deep generative models produce cardiac MR images together with segmentation masks, conditioned on patient clinical profiles.
  • Compared with an unconditioned baseline on anatomical fidelity, downstream utility and privacy.
  • Goal: ease data scarcity and privacy barriers that hold back medical AI.
PyTorchGenerative modelsSegmentationPrivacy evaluation

UDM-Net: Marine Plastic Debris Segmentation & Detection

Research collaboration with NILU (Norway) and NITTTR Kolkata.

  • Hybrid model: U-Net++ encoder-decoder plus a DINO detection head on a shared backbone, with multi-scale feature fusion and CBAM/SE attention.
  • Segments and detects floating plastic in a single pass.
  • Benchmarked against ten baselines: YOLOv11, YOLOv10, Faster R-CNN, EfficientDet, RetinaNet, RT-DETR, DINO, Mask R-CNN, DeepLabV3+ and U-Net++.
  • Two joint publications in preparation.
