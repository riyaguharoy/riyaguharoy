Master's Thesis · USN in collaboration with Simula Research Laboratory

2026 – Present

Hybrid Deep Learning for Marine Plastic Debris Segmentation and Detection

Research collaboration with NILU, Norway and NITTTR Kolkata
  • Building UDM-Net, a hybrid model combining a U-Net++ encoder-decoder with a DINO detection head, multi-scale feature fusion and CBAM/SE attention on a shared backbone to segment and detect floating plastic debris in a single pass.
  • Benchmarking against ten baselines: CNN detectors (YOLOv11, YOLOv10, Faster R-CNN, EfficientDet, RetinaNet), transformer detectors (RT-DETR, DINO), and segmentation models (Mask R-CNN, DeepLabV3+, U-Net++) on marine debris and microplastics datasets.
  • Work in progress toward two joint publications.
